### Breaking

- **Emberback Relay is replaced by Last Train Out (`last-train`).** Sessions and installed copies of `emberback` must be recreated; the old world ID has no alias. The new bundled world follows passengers on the last train, stopped in a tunnel under the river during a citywide blackout as water comes in. It ships with a new cover and five character portraits. See [bundled worlds](../en/builtin/index.md#the-four-built-in-worlds).
