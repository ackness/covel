### Added

- **The stage reads stories while they are written.** During a turn the stage reader shows each finished line of the draft, waits at the last line written so far (auto-play pauses there too) and starts again at the first line when the model revises. After the commit it stays at the line you are reading instead of replaying the story. It follows the "Show the story as it is written" setting. See [stage](../en/builtin/stage.md).
