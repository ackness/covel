### Added

- **Harukaze Academy now counts down to the festival with world time.** Festival Prep derives the days remaining from the clock, starting at 21, dropping by one each midnight and reaching zero on festival day. Rin and Yuma each gain a hidden route event and a follow-up, bringing the authored events to eight across all five routes.

### Changed

- **Harukaze Academy 0.3.4 has clearer campus periods and revised Chinese and English text.** Period labels distinguish classes, dismissal, club activities, dusk, and after closing. Exact lunch and dismissal times remain in the timetable because period boundaries accept only whole hours. Character cards, relationship rules, event briefs, dimension descriptions, and memory hints use more direct language while keeping the existing relationship records and numeric rules.

### Upgrade notes

- **Create a new Harukaze Academy session to use the revised starting data.** Existing sessions retain their imported characters, dimensions, and events; this update does not migrate them. See [world data](../reference/world-data.md) for the countdown and route-event structure.
