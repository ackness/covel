### Changed

- **The English editions of Haruka Academy, Mistport and Lantern Barrow read as natural English.** Every English text of the three Chinese worlds was translated again for meaning rather than word for word and checked line by line against the Chinese by a second reviewer. About 60 meaning errors are fixed, among them lines that said the opposite of the source (a tide stage labelled "Receded", a lesson "too cheap to call a monopoly"), a student committee chair called "Head Librarian", and a torch that burned "about an hour" where the world's clock counts in phases. Characters have distinct voices, and some player-facing labels are plainer (Haruka Academy's relationship stages are now Just met / Friends / Crush / On the verge / Together). Proper names are unchanged. Sessions created before this change keep the text they were created with.
- **Lantern Barrow's English edition has plainer names.** The world is now "Lantern Barrow: The First Delve", the keeper is the Lampkeeper (Lampkeeper Ysolde, the Lampkeeper's Cottage) and the marsh-king is the Marsh King. Sessions created before this change keep the names they were created with.

### Fixed

- **World rules fire on the words English players type.** The trigger words of the three worlds' rules and lorebook entries now cover the common English forms of each idea instead of literal phrases that almost never matched (for example "three bells", "appraised", "favoritism", "singing").
