### Changed

- **Breaking: agents complete only through validated output submission.** Invalid JSON, schema, story shape and review failures are corrected inside ordinary loop steps with independent bounded counters. Finalizers offer only the submission tool, and incomplete or content-only replies cannot become successful output. Replace `agent.loop.fallback: answer-without-tools` with `submit-output` and remove `truncation: accept`. Recreate suspended development runs written under the previous completion contract. See [agent completion](../en/reference/plugins.md#agent-output-submission) and [stop reasons](../en/reference/protocol.md#stop-reasons).
- **Review validated output with `ReviewAgentOutput`.** The hook receives the candidate, original call identity and public tool evidence, and can accept or request a bounded correction. Effects remain owned by successful business tools; output acceptance does not promise persistence. See [hook contract](../en/reference/hooks.md#reviewagentoutput).
