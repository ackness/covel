### Changed

- **Setup and failed turns have one clear recovery notice.** The notice explains the recorded cause, links to model settings when a key, permission, endpoint or rate limit needs attention, and keeps retry in one place. Header and composer labels no longer repeat the failure while recovery is visible; timeline summaries stay muted with expandable details. Setup execution records stay neutral after play starts or the page reloads. See [Built-in App Features](../en/builtin/features.md#retry).
