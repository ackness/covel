### Added

- **A failed dice check can cost something the player can see.** `dice-check` accepts `check.consequences@1` (`data/check-consequences.yaml`) and `check.clocks@1` (`data/check-clocks.yaml`). Each failed check pays the most specific cost it can still pay: an item taken through `inventory.change@1`, or segments on a clock shown in a new "Clocks" panel. Prose that leaves the cost out is sent back once. Rules: [built-in plugins · cost of failure](../en/builtin/rpg.md#cost-of-failure), fields: [world data](../en/reference/world-data.md#check-consequences).
- **Carried items help checks.** An inventory item can have `effects: { check: { attribute, modifier } }`. The paginated `inventory.bag@2` service reports it, and `dice-check` adds the best carried item for each attribute to its modifier. Custom providers and callers must switch to `inventory.bag@2` and support `offset`; saved bags do not need to be recreated. See [how items and relationships change a check](../en/builtin/rpg.md#how-items-and-relationships-change-a-check).
- **NPCs who have gone cold make checks harder.** `affinity` provides the read service `affinity.tier@1` (scores and tiers by name). A check whose receipt names an NPC below the neutral tier in its new optional `with` field is one difficulty step harder for each tier below neutral. A clock in `check.clocks@1` can also `raise` attributes once it is full. The guard and the recorder hold the receipt to the same modifiers and difficulties.
- **Quests and objectives can have deadlines.** `core-quest` reads `deadline: { when, label, missed }` on a quest or objective. `when` is a dimension condition, a world-time value, an NPC's affinity (`{ affinity: <name>, path: tier, in: [...] }`) or a turn number. The new pre-turn runtime `core-quest/deadline` fails overdue quests, marks missed objectives, and tells the narrator through `story-context@1`. See [quest deadlines](../en/builtin/rpg.md#quest-deadlines).
- **`world-time-context@1` carries `elapsedSinceStart`.** Time deadlines and hidden plot `time` conditions can count from the start of the story ([world time](../en/reference/world-time.md)).
- **Last Train uses all of it.** Failed checks there soak you, use up chalk, torches or phones, and raise the panic in the car. The torch helps wayfinding, the staff key mechanics and the staff jacket rapport. A full panic or cold clock makes some checks harder, and Graham walks off to the pump room if he goes cold on you. The world version is 2.2.0.

### Changed

- **`narrator` reads `story-context@1`.** Providers such as quest deadlines now reach the story prompt.
- **`check.resolved` validates attributes, item bonuses and difficulty.** A receipt must choose a listed attribute unless the modifier list is empty. Receipts that skip an item bonus or ignore a harder difficulty are sent back. The inventory ledger runs after check settlement so it does not charge the same cost again.
