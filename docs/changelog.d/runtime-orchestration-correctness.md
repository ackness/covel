### Fixed

- **Runtime scheduling and event chains enforce hooks, optional bindings, aborts, and effect hazards.** Manual and recovery batch scheduling honors `PreSchedule` hook filtering, DAG scheduling isolates independent dead cycles without falsely skipping optional or satisfied `cardinality: one` input bindings, `runEventChain` halts on mid-chain aborts and applies `COVEL_EFFECTS_POLICY`, `resumeSuspendedRuntime` builds fallback hook scopes from all active runtimes, and non-player late-setup completions attach `sessionClock` without advancing player turn counts.
- **Sub-agents preserve same-turn visibility and recover delegation budgets.** Delegated sub-agents inherit forked `pluginDataVisibility` / read-dependency tracking (merged only on live success), clear failed `loadingBudget` promises for retry, and avoid leaking `calls`/`actors` quotas on pre-start batch aborts.
- **Write-service locks, hook pipelines, and retry timers stay consistent under timeouts and malformed payloads.** Write-service provider mutexes remain held until non-cooperative handlers settle, `PostLLMResponse` preserves non-empty `correction` values across chained handlers while pinning immutable `PreStateCommit` envelopes and validating `replace` field shapes, and `llm-retry` guards against late post-settlement timer re-arming.
- **EventBus LRU eviction, RPC approval caches, durable job workers, and streaming slots prevent leaks and overlap.** `EventBus.pin().release()` maintains LRU map order and `invalidateReplay` avoids allocating state for untracked sessions; `RpcApprovalGate` bounds `sessionCache`, isolates multi-payload one-time grants, and sweeps stale pending approvals on read/decide; `runtime-job-worker` retains runtime concurrency slots until unwinding executions settle and caps `maxQueueMs` / credential handoff TTLs to 30 minutes (see [plugins](../en/reference/plugins.md#triggers)); `bootstrap/plugin-entry` isolates cross-session in-flight loads, prunes inactive sessions, and bounds `withSnapshot` retries; `model-relay` serializes SSE writes; and `ai-provider` releases `LLMSlot` before yielding final stream completion.
