### Fixed

- **Lantern Barrow's rules text gives the attribute modifier the dice actually use.** The world said checks were "d20 + attribute", which reads as +4 for an attribute of 4. Both dice plugins turn an attribute into a modifier over its declared range, so Lantern Barrow's 0–5 attributes give +2 per point (+10 at 5). The Chinese and English world text now say so; the difficulties 8/12/16/20 and the natural 20 / natural 1 rule are unchanged.
