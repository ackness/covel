### Changed

- **Stage mode reads a new story one line at a time.** The response panel opens with a text box that shows one paragraph or dialogue line, the speaker's nameplate and a progress count, and highlights the speaking character's portrait. Click, Space, Enter or → pages forward, ← goes back, and Esc or **Show all** shows the whole story. Auto-play turns each line after a delay that grows with its length. Choices and input stay available at every line.
